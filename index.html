What is this?

Public health is the work of keeping whole communities healthy — not just treating people one at a time, but understanding why some neighborhoods, jobs, or groups face more risk than others, and what actually helps.

This is a playful way into some of the ideas behind that work. Each track connects to a real concept — how diseases spread, why where you live affects your health, how technology helps or hurts. No background needed.
